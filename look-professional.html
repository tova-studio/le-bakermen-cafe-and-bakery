About us

Your cosy neighbourhood café in Wigram

Welcome to Le Bakermen, your cosy neighbourhood café nestled in the heart of Wigram at 187 Wigram Road, Christchurch.

We’re passionate about serving up handcrafted coffee, delicious baked treats and wholesome meals made with love. Everything we do comes back to three simple things: great drinks, fresh bakes and feel-good food made with care.

Le Bakermen offers the perfect space to unwind and enjoy delicious, handcrafted flavour. Stop in for a coffee and something from the cabinet, or take a seat and slow down for a while.

Our menu

What can I get at Le Bakermen?

Drinks, bakes and food, each made with care. Here’s what you’ll find when you visit us on Wigram Road.

  • Great drinks

    Handcrafted coffee and great drinks, made with care, whether it’s your morning cup or an afternoon pick-me-up.

  • Fresh bakes

    Delicious baked treats from our bakery: flaky Danishes, scrolls, brioche and croissants, laid out in the pastry cabinet.

  • Feel-good food

    Wholesome meals made with love. It’s food to enjoy at the table while you take a moment to unwind.
